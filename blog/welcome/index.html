Welcome to the Rebellion.

Hey. I'm Daphne — and Closet Rebel exists because somewhere along the way, women in my generation got handed a really small box.

Here's what was supposed to be in it: beige. Sensible. Slimming. "Age appropriate." Comfortable shoes, but not the fun kind. Hide the arms. Don't show too much chest. Don't draw attention. Don't be too colorful, too loud, too much.

Here's what wasn't in it: us.

Who Closet Rebel is for

Gen X women. Older. Plus size welcome and centered. Women in midlife who are done shrinking. Women who realized somewhere around 42 that "rules" were just other people's preferences with a marketing budget.

If you've ever stared into your closet at 7am and felt invisible — this is for you.

If you've ever loved a piece in the store and put it back because someone might think it's "not for your age" — this is for you.

If you've ever wanted to wear hot pink to your kid's school event and chickened out — this is so for you.

"Wear the life you want. Wake up in it long enough and you become her."

What you'll find here

Outfit ideas in real plus sizes. Color theory you can use. Affiliate finds I actually like (Amazon, ShopMy, Etsy). Manifestation through what you wear — yes, I really mean that. The science is on our side.

And every Sunday: a recap of the week's best finds straight to your inbox if you join the Inner Circle.

The one rule we never break

If it makes you feel like you're hiding, take it off.

That's it. That's the whole rebellion.

Ready?
