Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: May 2026

Closet Rebel Official ("we", "us", "the site") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Email addresses you give us when you join the Inner Circle email list.
  • Basic site analytics — pages visited, referrer, device type, country. We do not collect personally identifying browsing data beyond standard analytics.
  • Comments and replies you submit on the site or our connected social profiles.

We do not collect, store, or sell sensitive personal information. We do not run ads, sell data to third parties, or build profiles of our readers.

How we use it

  • To send the Inner Circle weekly digest to subscribers who opted in.
  • To improve the site (which posts are popular, which links work, where readers come from).
  • To reply when you contact us.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site (Amazon, ShopMy, Etsy, and others) are affiliate links. If you click through and buy, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This funds the site. It never changes what we recommend.

Third-party services

We use the following third parties to run the site and our publishing pipeline:

  • Google Analytics — site traffic measurement
  • Google Sheets — editorial calendar (private, internal)
  • Make.com — automated cross-posting to social platforms
  • Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube — public profiles where we publish
  • Buy Me a Coffee — voluntary tip jar
  • Hostinger — website hosting

Each of these services has its own privacy policy that governs the data they collect. We only share with them what's needed to deliver the service.

Pinterest API specifically

Closet Rebel Official's developer app uses the Pinterest API only to publish original Pin content from our editorial calendar to our own boards (@daphnerjames). We do not access, store, or share data from any other Pinterest user. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on our automation server and used only to publish on our own behalf.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe from the Inner Circle email at any time using the link in any email.
  • Request a copy of any data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing closetrebelofficial@gmail.com.
  • If you're in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA. Email us and we'll honor them.

Cookies

The site uses minimal cookies for analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the site's core functionality.

Children

Closet Rebel Official is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a minor has shared data with us, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in the Inner Circle email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email closetrebelofficial@gmail.com.
